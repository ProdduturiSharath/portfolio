Privacy & analytics.

This portfolio is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Contact messages are submitted through Formspree when you choose to use the contact form.

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics may collect portfolio usage information such as visits, approximate engagement, project opens, résumé clicks, and successful contact actions. Analytics is blocked until you select “Allow analytics.”

Analytics events sent from this site use predefined values only. Contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, and message content are not sent to Google Analytics.

You can decline analytics or change your choice using “Analytics preferences” in the site footer. Turning analytics off removes this portfolio’s analytics cookies and refreshes the page.
